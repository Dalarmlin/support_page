常見問題

如何新增倒數日？
在主畫面點右下角的「＋」，輸入標題與日期後儲存即可。

如何把倒數日加到桌面小工具？
長按主畫面空白處 → 加入小工具 → 搜尋「時光倒數」，即可將最接近的倒數日顯示在桌面。

資料會上傳雲端嗎？
不會。所有紀念日資料都只儲存在你的裝置本機。

如何開啟提醒通知？
新增或編輯倒數日時開啟提醒即可；請確認已於系統設定允許本 App 傳送通知。

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a countdown?
Tap the "+" button on the main screen, enter a title and date, then save.

How do I add a countdown to the home screen widget?
Long-press an empty area of your home screen → Add Widget → search for "TimeCountdown".

Is my data uploaded to the cloud?
No. All your data is stored locally on your device only.

How do I enable reminders?
Turn on the reminder when adding or editing a countdown, and make sure notifications are allowed for this app in system settings.
