這個 App 做什麼

紫微攻略會依你的出生資料排出紫微斗數命盤，再和你身邊每個人（主管、同事、下屬、曖昧對象、伴侶或配偶、前任）的生年四化交叉比對，結合流年、流月、流日，由 AI 分析今天彼此最容易摩擦或互助的地方，並給你具體的相處建議。

常見問題

需要註冊或登入嗎？

不需要。開啟即可使用，不必建立帳號。

如何建立我的命盤？

在首頁點「建立本命資料」，輸入出生日期、時辰與出生地。App 會以出生地經度校正為真太陽時。定位權限只用來帶入出生地經度，可以不開啟，改為手動選擇。

如何新增要分析的人？

在「命盤設定」分頁新增關係人，選擇職場（主管、同事、下屬）或感情（曖昧暗戀、伴侶配偶、前任）類別並輸入對方生日。已婚的夫妻相處也適用感情模式。

我的資料會被上傳嗎？

你的生日、命盤與關係人清單只存在你的裝置上。使用 AI 分析時，只會傳送該次分析所需的命盤結構資料與你輸入的問題，人名一律以 T1、T2 等代號取代，透過加密連線送至 Google Gemini API。詳見 App 內「設定 → 隱私權政策」。

每天可以用幾次？

免費版的今日雷達掃描、五維碰撞解算、隨身提問每天各 1 次，每類關係人可新增 1 人。VIP 每天各 10 次，每類最多 10 人。次數於每天午夜（裝置時間）重置。

如何訂閱、取消或管理 VIP？

VIP 提供週費、月費、年費三種自動續訂方案。要取消或變更方案，請到 iPhone「設定 → 你的名字 → 訂閱」，選擇本 App 管理。請在目前週期結束前至少 24 小時取消，以免自動續訂。我們無法直接替你退款，退款請透過 Apple 回報問題申請。

換手機或重新安裝後，VIP 不見了？

請使用同一個 Apple ID 登入，到 App「設定 → 還原購買」，或在 VIP 頁面點「還原購買」。

換手機時資料會保留嗎？

命盤與關係人資料會隨 iCloud 裝置備份（Android 為 Google 備份）一起轉移。請先確認舊手機的系統備份已開啟。

忘記 App 鎖的 PIN 碼怎麼辦？

PIN 碼只存在你的裝置上，我們無法替你重設。若有開啟 Face ID／指紋，可直接用生物辨識解鎖。否則只能刪除並重新安裝 App，本機資料會一併清除。

為什麼會看到廣告？

免費版由 Google AdMob 廣告支持，首頁底部會顯示橫幅廣告。升級 VIP 即完全無廣告。

分析結果準確嗎？

內容依紫微斗數命理推演並由 AI 生成，僅供參考與娛樂，不構成任何專業建議。重要決定請依實際情況審慎判斷。

如何切換語言？

到「設定 → 語言」，可選擇繁體中文或 English。

English

What this app does

Ziwei: Career & Love builds your Zi Wei Dou Shu birth chart and cross-references it with the birth-year Si-Hua (Four Transformations) of the people around you: boss, coworkers, direct reports, a crush, your partner or spouse, or an ex. Combined with the year, month and day cycles, the AI shows where friction or support is most likely today and gives practical advice.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. No sign-in required.

How do I create my chart?

On the Home tab, tap "Create natal profile" and enter your birth date, hour and birthplace. The app corrects to true solar time using the birthplace longitude. Location access is optional and only fills in that longitude; you can pick the place manually instead.

How do I add people?

In the Charts tab, add a person, choose Work (boss, coworker, direct report) or Love (crush, partner or spouse, ex) and enter their birthday. Love mode also covers married couples.

Is my data uploaded?

Your birth details, chart and people list stay on your device. For an AI reading, only the structured chart data needed for that request and any question you type are sent, over an encrypted connection, to the Google Gemini API. Names are replaced with codes such as T1 and T2. See Settings → Privacy Policy in the app for details.

How many readings do I get per day?

Free: 1 radar scan, 1 five-dimension reading and 1 quick question per day, and 1 person per category. VIP: 10 of each per day and up to 10 people per category. Limits reset at midnight device time.

How do I subscribe, cancel or manage VIP?

VIP comes as weekly, monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscriptions. To cancel or change plans, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions and select this app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid renewal. We cannot issue refunds directly; request one through Apple's Report a Problem.

VIP is missing after reinstalling or switching phones

Sign in with the same Apple ID, then tap Settings → Restore purchase in the app, or Restore purchase on the VIP screen.

Will my data move to a new phone?

Your chart and people list are included in iCloud device backup (Google backup on Android). Make sure system backup is on for the old phone.

I forgot my app lock PIN

The PIN is stored only on your device, so we cannot reset it. If Face ID or fingerprint unlock is on, use that. Otherwise, delete and reinstall the app; this erases the data on the device.

Why are there ads?

The free version is supported by Google AdMob, with a banner at the bottom of the Home screen. VIP removes all ads.

Are the readings accurate?

Readings are based on Zi Wei Dou Shu and generated by AI. They are for reference and entertainment only and are not professional advice.

How do I change the language?

Go to Settings → Language and choose 繁體中文 or English.
