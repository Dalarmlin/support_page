Super Picture Clash Support

Local photo party game for iPhone and iPad. One to four players around one device. Pictures and scores stay on the phone.

Contact

For bugs, camera or photo-library questions, and store-listing issues, open a GitHub issue. That is the support channel for this app. No account is required to read existing threads.

Developer: BRAI Lab

How to play

  • Snap or pick photos for fighters, optional bosses, and the arena.
  • Lay the phone flat in the middle of the table. Each person sits at a corner.
  • Fighters spin on their own. Tap GO to dash and use the action button to strike. Knock rivals off the round arena to score.
  • Three rounds. Round 3 is the boss fight. Empty corners can be filled by CPU.

Custom stage and bosses

You design the ring and the bosses. Photograph any surface around you (a blackboard, table, carpet, poster, or living-room floor) and it becomes the arena. Use a different picture each round, or the same one for all three. Pick any photos you like for the big boss and mini bosses: a doodle, a toy, a pet, a game character, or a friend. There is no fixed villain.

Common questions

Photos look missing. Allow Camera and Photos in iOS Settings → Super Picture Clash. You can still play with empty slots.

Are pictures uploaded? No. Fighter, boss, and arena photos are copied only onto this device. Match history stays local. There is no game server and no account.

Ads. The game is free and may show Google AdMob ads. Ad requests are separate from your photos and scores. You can refuse App Tracking Transparency if iOS asks.

Age. General audience. Not directed at children under 13.
